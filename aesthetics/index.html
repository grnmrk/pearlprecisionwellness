Aesthetics

I'm excited to offer my cosmetic services through Belén Aesthetics, where I have the opportunity to work alongside an extremely talented team of providers and offer a wide range of advanced aesthetic treatments.

Overview

Clinical Aesthetics,
Thoughtfully Done

Our aesthetic offerings focus on skin quality, rejuvenation, and refined cosmetic dermatology procedures. Care is designed for patients who want results informed by a deep understanding of how skin works from the surface and from within. Injectables such as filler and Botox are part of the menu. Regenerative treatments include PRP, Sculptra, microneedling, and laser.

  • Laser Treatments | Skin rejuvenation, pigmentation, redness, and texture
  • Neuromodulators | Botox®, Dysport® & Daxxify®
  • Dermal Fillers | Facial balancing, contouring, and volume restoration
  • Microneedling | Collagen stimulation and skin rejuvenation
  • Regenerative Aesthetics | PRP, PRF & PDGF treatments
