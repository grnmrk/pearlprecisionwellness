Overview

Skin Care With
Root-Cause Depth

Functional dermatology looks at chronic and complex skin conditions through both a clinical dermatology lens and a functional medicine framework. Visits address what is happening on the skin while also exploring nutrition, gut health, hormones, lifestyle, and other contributors.

  • Acne, rosacea, eczema, and psoriasis
  • Hair loss and chronic itching
  • Recurrent rashes and hives
  • Skin spot checks and comprehensive screenings
  • Medical dermatology visits for traditional care
